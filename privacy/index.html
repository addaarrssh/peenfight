12. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as it is needed for the specified purpose, or as required by law, whichever is longer.

  • Match Records and ranking rows: for as long as the Service is operated in a form that depends on a historical ranking, or until we grant an erasure request that the law permits us to grant.
  • Signed-in snapshot: until you ask us to erase it, you close the binding, or the account is otherwise closed.
  • Local save: until you clear site data or the browser evicts it.
  • Analytics and session replay: according to the retention settings of those products, typically a rolling window of months.
  • Server and security logs: a short operational window, and in any event not longer than needed for security, debugging, and legal claims, subject to clause 13.2.
  • Correspondence and grievance files: for the period needed to handle the request and to meet legal limitation periods.
  • After cancellation of a registration: where the IT Rules, 2021 require it, registration information is retained for one hundred and eighty days after cancellation or withdrawal.

When we no longer need personal data, we delete it or irreversibly de-identify it, except where a law requires us to keep it.
