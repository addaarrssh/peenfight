⚡ 5 GAME RULES ⚡

Simple rules for desk pen fight battles.

1
RULE #1

TABLE KNOCKOUT

Pen falls off desk = Out.

"Keep your pen on the table."
2
RULE #2

SINGLE FLICK

One finger flick per turn. No pushing or dragging.

"Aim for the center of mass."
3
RULE #3

NO TABLE SHAKING

Shaking or tilting the table is a foul.

"Keep the desk still."
FOUL! ⛔
4
RULE #4

DEFENSE & GRIP

High friction pens stop closer to edges.

"Sideways pens resist incoming hits."
5
RULE #5

WINNER TAKES MATCH

Knock the opponent off the desk to win.

"Last pen standing wins."
